## 🔒 The Simple Version

**ScamGuard collects minimal information and deletes it quickly.**

- ✅ We collect: Email, age verification, and scam descriptions you provide
- ❌ We don't collect: Banking info, credit cards, government IDs, health data, location
- 📅 We keep it for: 30 days, then permanently delete it

## ❓ COMMON QUESTIONS

### Q: Does ScamGuard sell my data?
**A:** No. We have no financial incentive to sell data. We don't even offer that service.

### Q: Who can see my scam description?
**A:** Only:
- You (in your account)
- Our AI system (for analysis)
- ScamGuard security staff (only if investigating a problem)
- NOT advertisers, NOT other companies, NOT anyone else

### Q: Where is my data stored?
**A:** Primarily in Canada with encrypted copies in the USA (for backup).

### Q: Can the US government access my data?
**A:** Technically possible (under US law), but we minimize risk by:
- Encrypting it (hard to read if accessed)
- Deleting it after 30 days (less time to access)
- Challenging requests when legally possible

### Q: What if ScamGuard gets hacked?
**A:** We notify you within 72 hours. We explain what was accessed, what we're doing, and what you should do.

### Q: Can I use ScamGuard without giving consent?
**A:** No. Consent is required to process your scam descriptions. But you can withdraw consent anytime (Section: Your Rights).

### Q: What happens to my gamification data (XP, badges)?
**A:** That's non-personal data (doesn't identify you) and we keep it for your experience/motivation. You can delete it anytime.

### Q: Do you use cookies to track me?
**A:** Only functional cookies (to keep you logged in). We don't use tracking cookies or ads.

### Q: How often do you update this policy?
**A:** We review annually (next: February 2027). If major changes, we email you 30 days ahead.
