## 4. AREAS REQUIRING ONGOING MONITORING

While ScamGuard is legally compliant for launch, counsel recommends monitoring these areas:

### 4.1 US Government Access to Data ⚠️

**Issue:** OpenAI infrastructure in USA; subject to FISA, NSLs
**Current Mitigation:**
- Encryption (limits usefulness if accessed)
- 30-day deletion (limits exposure window)
- DPA commitment to challenge requests

**Monitoring Required:**
- [ ] Monitor US law changes (FISA, privacy laws)
- [ ] Evaluate alternative processors if US law becomes more restrictive
- [ ] Consider additional encryption (homomorphic encryption) for Phase 2

**Assessment:** Acceptable with current mitigations. Keep under review.

### 4.2 Age Verification Effectiveness ⚠️

**Issue:** Age verification is self-reported (honor system)
**Current Mitigation:**
- ConsentBanner requires age confirmation
- Terms of Service state 18+ requirement
- Monitoring for unusual sign-up patterns

**Monitoring Required:**
- [ ] Monitor analytics for child sign-ups
- [ ] Annual review of COPPA implications (US minor protection)
- [ ] Phase 2: Implement government ID verification

**Assessment:** Acceptable for MVP. Improve in Phase 2.

### 4.3 OpenAI Policy Changes ⚠️

**Issue:** OpenAI may change terms or data policies
**Current Mitigation:**
- DPA explicitly prohibits training use
- 30-day data deletion limits liability
- Alternative providers identified (Claude, Gemini)

**Monitoring Required:**
- [ ] Quarterly review of OpenAI terms changes
- [ ] Annual compliance audit
- [ ] Maintain ability to switch providers within 2 weeks

**Assessment:** Well-mitigated. Continue quarterly monitoring.

### 4.4 Regulatory Changes (Loi 25 Implementation) ⚠️

**Issue:** Loi 25 is Quebec's newest data protection law; guidance may evolve
**Current Mitigation:**
- ScamGuard exceeds minimum Loi 25 requirements
- DPO positioned to adapt to guidance
- Documentation comprehensive

**Monitoring Required:**
- [ ] Follow CAI (Quebec data protection commission) guidance
- [ ] Participate in industry working groups
- [ ] Update policies as CAI issues interpretations

**Assessment:** Proactive approach recommended. No compliance risk at launch.
