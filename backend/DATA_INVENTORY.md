**Framework:** Loi 25 (Quebec GDPR), PIPEDA, GDPR

## MASTER DATA INVENTORY

### Format
Each row contains:
- **Data Type:** What data
- **PII Status:** Is it personally identifiable?
- **Collection Point:** Where collected
- **Storage Location:** Where stored
- **Retention Period:** How long kept
- **Purpose:** Why collected
- **Legal Basis:** Why allowed to collect
- **Encryption:** How protected
- **Processor:** Who can access
