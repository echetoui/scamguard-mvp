## Template 1: Privacy Notice (Welcome Email)

### Subject: Welcome to ScamGuard - Your Privacy & Security Matter

```
Bonjour [User First Name],

Welcome to ScamGuard! We're excited to help you detect scams and protect yourself.

Your Privacy is Our Priority
At ScamGuard, we take your privacy seriously. We comply with Quebec's Loi 25
(Quebec GDPR) and PIPEDA. Here's what you need to know:

What Data We Collect:
• Your text messages and emails for analysis
• Your scam detection scores (to help you learn)
• Your consent preferences

What We DON'T Collect:
• Your banking credentials
• Your personal contacts
• Your location data
• Any information you don't want to share

Your Rights:
✅ Access your data anytime
✅ Delete your data whenever you want
✅ Export your data in a readable format
✅ Ask questions about how we use your data

Need Help?
Our Data Protection Officer (DPO) is here to help:
Email: privacy@scamguard.ca
Response time: 5 business days (urgent: 24 hours)

Full Privacy Policy: [LINK]

Thank you for trusting ScamGuard!

ScamGuard Team
```

## Template 6: DPO Introduction to Users

### Subject: Meet Your Data Protection Officer

```
Bonjour ScamGuard Community,

We're pleased to introduce your Data Protection Officer (DPO) - a dedicated
professional committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring we comply
with all applicable data protection laws.

Who Is the DPO?
The DPO is responsible for:
• Monitoring our compliance with privacy laws (Loi 25, GDPR, PIPEDA)
• Handling your privacy requests (access, deletion, export)
• Investigating data breaches
• Conducting privacy audits
• Answering your privacy questions

How to Contact Your DPO:
Email: privacy@scamguard.ca
Phone: [TBD]
Response time: 5 business days (urgent: 24 hours)

What Your DPO Can Help With:
✅ "How is my data being used?" - Request a data access report
✅ "I want to delete my account" - Request data deletion
✅ "I don't agree with your privacy policy" - File a complaint
✅ "Do you have a partnership with [company]?" - Ask about data sharing
✅ "How long do you keep my data?" - Learn about retention policies

Your DPO Is Independent
Your DPO works independently from the rest of ScamGuard and reports directly
to our Executive Leadership. This ensures your privacy concerns are always
heard and taken seriously.

Your DPO Cannot:
❌ Be fired for protecting your privacy
❌ Be pressured to ignore privacy concerns
❌ Share your personal information
❌ Take sides with the company over your rights

We're Here to Help
Your privacy matters to us. Please don't hesitate to reach out with questions
or concerns.

Best regards,

ScamGuard Data Protection Officer
privacy@scamguard.ca
```
